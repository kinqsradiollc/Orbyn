- **Screen/URL/viewport:** Settings → AI connections & models; `http://127.0.0.1:5174/app`;390×844 CSS px; Light appearance; capture source d1015183 (shared desktop/mobile UI files unchanged through current HEAD f0624e9d).
- **Reproduction:** Open Settings, expand AI connections & models, scroll to ChatGPT. Observe “Direct web sign-in is unavailable. Manage your connected accounts below.” Continue through the visible ChatGPT section.
- **Expected:** When direct web sign-in is unavailable, helper copy points only to an account-management control that exists in this view, or states the connection limitation without implying such a control is below.

No connected-account management control appears there. The only management link is for usage. No Connect button or connection flow is exposed.
