# Edit prose without altering its evidence

Identify the reader, requested outcome and statements whose wording carries technical or evidential meaning. Preserve measured quantities, release state, attribution, uncertainty and the user's intended voice before shortening anything.

Edit one passage around its main point. Replace vague claims with supported actions or outcomes, remove duplicated setup and choose paragraph breaks that follow the reasoning. Keep terminology consistent with the owning product or API contract. Do not manufacture a numerical benefit to make a sentence concrete.

Read the complete result for meaning, not just prohibited words. “The export is ready” may be shorter but false if only HTML source was generated; retain the missing PDF inspection. A user's intentional humor or informal phrasing is not automatically filler.

Return the requested edited copy. Add an evidence note only where a factual gap affects publication. Product labels, social strategy and visual composition retain their respective owners. Word-count reduction alone is not a quality measure.
