# Widget Events contract

A registration is identified by (topic, key), not callback equality. Subscribing
again with the same identity replaces its callback: later emits deliver once to
the latest callback. Distinct keys or topics remain independent even when they
share the exact same callable. A disposer belongs to the registration returned by
its subscribe call, is safe to repeat and must not remove a newer replacement.
Emit dispatches the snapshot current at emit start. Nested subscription changes
must not mutate that in-progress iteration.

Keep this target-owned skill and its host links in place. The public portable
library does not own Widget's contract or its dirty working documents.
