# Compare two falsifiable candidates

Write the decision in one sentence and freeze the inputs, hard constraints and scoring observations before making alternatives. Prefer two sketches that differ on the uncertain design choice. Give each a separate path; shared generated files and databases need their own owner.

Use a comparison table with constraint, candidate A evidence, candidate B evidence and remaining uncertainty. A hard constraint disqualifies a candidate before aesthetic or convenience scoring. For reconnect delivery, feed both candidates the same disconnect, duplicate and out-of-order trace; a screenshot cannot settle delivery correctness.

Inspect each artifact rather than trusting its author's summary. Select one coherent contract, or report that neither meets the criteria. Preserve a useful rejected rationale without merging incompatible lifecycles. If the host cannot supply permitted delegates, compare sequential sketches directly and disclose that the review was not independent.

Stop after the decision is supported. Do not create a tournament or claim two agreeing agents verified correctness. Return the selected artifact, why it won and the smallest unresolved check to the architecture or implementation owner.
