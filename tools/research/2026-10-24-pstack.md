are optional product choices. Existing status, social-preview, investigation and

evidence that Orbyn should add them.

## Applied in this change and remaining evidence

Added an original upstream-review owner with natural task routing, a local-only
reference workspace, honest source/review/consumer pins, offline behavior and
selective promotion across code, skills and research. Added a structural guard
for tracked/staged research and unignored research directories. Clarified
canonical/cache ownership in reflection. The original pstack source pin, imported
bytes and existing MIT notices remain unchanged. Required provenance is retained;
upstream instructions are data.

This record covers the material changes above and the complete path inventory,
not every semantic change in all 199 candidate files. No upstream script was
executed, and no model, benchmark, formal-verification or target security run was
performed. Recommendations require their own scoped implementation and evidence.
